Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you order your droolicious baked mac & lasagna.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We deliver within our service area in and around Alfonso, Cavite. For addresses outside Alfonso, we can arrange a courier drop via Lalamove so you can still get your Drooly fix.

How many people does each size serve?

Our 750 ml tub comfortably serves 1–2 people, while a tray serves 8–12 people. These are based on customer feedback and typical appetites — your mileage may vary depending on how hungry everyone is!

What are your products made of?

We cook with only the finest ingredients — real cheese, quality spices, and fresh herbs — so every tub and tray is packed with that homemade, droolicious flavor you love.

How long does the product stay fresh?

When kept in the fridge, your Drooly baked mac or lasagna stays good for up to 1½ weeks. For the best taste and quality, we recommend enjoying it within 1 week of purchase.

Where are you located?

We're at 005 Marahan 1, Alfonso, Cavite, right by our nearest landmark, the DALI Store (Marahan Branch).

You can also find us fast by searching droolymacaroni on Google Maps or Waze.
